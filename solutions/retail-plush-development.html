Retail Plush Development

Production-ready plush toys for retail shelves. Full safety compliance, retail packaging, and scalable manufacturing.

Ready for Retail

Retail plush requires a different standard than promotional items. Safety certifications, hang tags, barcode labels, and retail-grade packaging — we handle it all.

Whether it's a seasonal collection for a major retailer or a limited-edition collaboration, we scale from hundreds to hundreds of thousands.

  • CPSIA & EN71 safety compliance
  • ASTM F963 certified materials
  • Custom retail packaging & hang tags
  • Barcode & UPC labeling
  • Scalable from 1,000 to 100,000+ units
